How to play Cryptogram

Every letter has been swapped for another one, consistently, all the way through the quote. Tap a coded letter, then tap the letter you think it stands for — every copy of it updates at once.

Start with the short words. A one-letter word is almost always A or I. The most common letter is usually E. Look for THE, and for a letter after an apostrophe — that is usually S or T.

Amber means you have used the same letter twice. Since no two coded letters share an answer, one of them is wrong.
