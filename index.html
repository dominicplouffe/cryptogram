    How to play

    Cryptogram. Every letter has been swapped for another one, consistently, all the way through the quote. Tap a coded letter, then tap the letter you think it stands for — every copy updates at once. Start with the short words: a one-letter word is almost always A or I, and the most common letter is usually E.

    Fiver. Guess a five-letter word in six tries. After each guess, green means the letter is in the right place, amber means it is in the word but somewhere else, and grey means it is not in the word at all.

    Missing Vowels. Every A, E, I, O and U has been taken out of a quote. Put them back. Y is left alone.

    Your daily streak counts consecutive days you finish at least one daily puzzle. Free play does not count toward it.
