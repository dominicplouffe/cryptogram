Privacy Policy — Plouffe Word Games

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Plouffe Word Games is a collection of word puzzles for iOS and Android made by Dominic Plouffe. The short version: the app collects no data at all. There is no long version hiding anything — the rest of this page just spells it out.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and it contains no third-party code that would do so on our behalf.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, streaks, statistics, and settings are saved on your device so the app can remember where you left off. That information never leaves your device: the app makes no network connections and works entirely offline. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Sharing

If you tap the share button after solving a puzzle, the app hands a small spoiler-free result card to your device's standard share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to share it with, and we never see it either way.

App stores

Downloading the app through the Apple App Store or Google Play involves those platforms in the ordinary way (for example, processing the download and any ratings you leave). That is governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies; we receive no personal information from it.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above — for example, by adding an optional online feature — this policy will be updated first, and the change will be stated plainly here with a new effective date.
